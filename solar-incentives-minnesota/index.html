Minnesota solar — common questions

Is the 30% federal solar tax credit available in Minnesota in 2026?

No — the federal Residential Clean Energy Credit ended for systems placed in service after December 31, 2025 (IRS), in every state including Minnesota. Minnesota does have a program on the books — MN solar credit for municipal utility / co-op customers (official source) — but it is not included in the estimate above: Only for customers of municipal utilities or electric co-ops (not investor-owned utilities), and the cited source is pending legislation — verify before relying on it.

What does solar cost in Minnesota in 2026?

Minnesota systems average $3.20 per watt before incentives (EnergySage marketplace, Jul 2026). A typical system sized for a $200/mo bill (10.8 kW) runs about $34,560 gross.

Is solar worth it in Minnesota without the federal credit?
