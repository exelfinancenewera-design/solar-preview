Key finding: With the federal 30% Residential Clean Energy Credit unavailable after Dec 31, 2025 (IRS), estimated payback for a $200/mo household now ranges from 2.2 years in Hawaii to 16.2 years in Iowa — driven almost entirely by state electricity rates (EIA, May 2026) and installed cost (EnergySage, Jul 2026). Methodology: 0.80 performance ratio, 0.5%/yr degradation, savings capped at usage, no export credit.
